Notes on nativeness:

Many Albanian basic nouns (nënë, baba, burrë, grua, bukë, diell, ujë, gur, sy, zog, peshk, pemë) are inherited Indo-European forms and count as the oldest/native vocabulary.

For colours, older native terms exist:

i kuq (native)

i verdhë (native)

kaltër is the older native term for “blue,” while blu is a more recent international borrowing. I have listed both, with kaltër noted as native.

Important Notes on Brahui Vocabulary

Brahui retains native Dravidian grammar, but lexicon is heavily replaced by centuries of contact with Balochi, Persian, and Urdu.

Only some core items (fire, stone, water, eye, nose, tree, grandmother) retain clearly Dravidian origins.

Basic colour terms in Brahui today are almost entirely Persian loans; genuine ancient native colour words are not attested.
